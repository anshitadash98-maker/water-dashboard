About This Project

Why we built India Water Watch

🎯 The Problem

India faces a serious groundwater crisis — many regions are extracting water faster than it can naturally recharge. Farmers, planners, and citizens often lack easy access to clear, region-wise data to understand the scale of the issue.

💡 Our Approach

India Water Watch brings together groundwater levels, district-wise monitoring, and state classifications (Safe, Semi-Critical, Critical) into one simple dashboard — making the data easier to explore and understand at a glance.

🛠️ Built With

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend, Chart.js for data visualization, and amCharts for the interactive state map. Built as part of a Smart India Hackathon prototype.

📊 Data Note

Current figures shown across the dashboard are sample/illustrative data, used to demonstrate the platform's design and functionality. Future versions aim to integrate live data from sources like India-WRIS and INGRES.
