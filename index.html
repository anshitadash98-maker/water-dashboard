Why This Matters

India draws more groundwater than any other country in the world. Many regions are extracting water faster than it naturally recharges, putting farming, drinking water, and long-term sustainability at risk. Clear, accessible data is the first step toward better decisions — for farmers, planners, and policymakers alike.
